Site & Organic OSM Way 491040107 Plot

Centred on Institute of Technical Education & Research (SOA University) in Jagamara, Khandagiri, Bhubaneswar (20.24830°N, 85.80127°E), this Pocket City maquette hugs the real OpenStreetMap campus parcel, eastern Jagamara stream, and southern hostel precinct from ITER Main Gate down to Boys Hostel 12.

Map- & Satellite-Calibrated Modelling: Every building footprint, road bend, gate location, median palm planter, parking lot, and wooded grove has been arranged directly from the OSM Way 491040107 map, Google Maps satellite imagery, and campus photographs with per-part 3D depth sorting so buildings never overlap. Gate lettering (ITER | SIKSHA 'O' ANUSANDHAN and ITER MAIN GATE · SOA) and A-Block's bold red ITER rooftop sign are projected in true 3D perspective.

Left-Hand Indian Campus Circulation

Following Indian left-hand traffic rules and the one-way boulevard arrows from ITER Main Gate, entering traffic runs south along the palm-lined split boulevard to the central roundabout, passes Central Library and ITER College Workshop, loops east along F block Rd to Gate 2 (Ln 6), and exits north along the western branch.

Hand-Built Campus Landmarks (All 57 Campus Structures)

  • L-Block (New 6-Storey Academic Complex), B-Block & 2026 Additions: Includes the new 6-storey L-shaped L-Block (CL-series lecture halls north of F-Block), G-Block, the EV Charging Station beside F/G-Block, the Centre for Nanoscience & Nanotechnology (CNN), ITER Admission Cell (A-Block), and indoor 2026 hubs including the Tech Mahindra Centre of Excellence and Centre for Quantum Science & Technology.
  • AIC SOA Building (Atal Incubation Centre) & S-Block Indoor Basketball Court: Positioned directly south of C-Block and west of F block Rd; features its dark-grey north wing, signature wide silver-grey gabled metal hall roof with central ridge (covering the 2nd-floor Indoor Basketball Court and 1st-floor NITI Aayog-supported AIC-SOA hub), west concrete courtyard wing, and east entry block.
  • ITER Central Library, College Workshop, GYM ITER & Physics Dept (D-Block): Rebuilt from close-up satellite imagery: Central Library with its two-tier stepped white-parapet roof and rooftop water-tank clusters; the elongated pale-cream pitched metal Workshop hall with its northern blue-grey extension roof leading to GYM, ITER; E-courtyard Physics/ECE D-Block; and Bank of India ATM at the SE plaza.
  • Full-Scale Ladies Hostel Compound (LH-1, LH-2, LH-3, LH-4 & Gate LH): Sprawling walled Girls Hostel campus spanning the south-west quadrant along ITER Girls Hostel Rd—entered via Gate LH on the western approach road and linking the tilted LH-1 wing, the wide northern LH-4 wing, the massive multi-courtyard LH-2 Hostel (with south-west tiled plaza & skybridge), and the tall east-side LH-3 wing alongside the Circular Building (Rotunda).
  • Full-Scale Boys Hostel Compound (Gate BH, BH-05, BH-6, BH-8, BH-10 & BH-12): Massive walled Boys Hostel enclave entered through Gate BH at the northern throat of ITER Boys Hostel Rd (off F block Rd), housing BH-6 and Boys Hostel 8 to the north-east, the wide horizontal ITER BH-05 and twin-courtyard BH-10 to the south-east, and the tall vertical north–south Boys Hostel 12 (BH-12) complex with its terracotta central courtyard to the south-west.

Bhubaneswar Tropical Climate Regimes

Bhubaneswar follows four distinct India Meteorological Department (IMD) regimes on Odisha's coastal plain:

  • Winter Dry (Jan–Feb): Cool, sunny, dry (~15–29°C, ~25 mm average rain). Crisp architectural shadows and active sports courts.
  • Pre-Monsoon Heat (Mar–May): Peak summer heat (up to 41°C) with occasional Kalbaisakhi squalls. Golden lawns and fiery scarlet Krishnachuda + pink-purple Patuli blooms.
  • South-West Monsoon (Jun–Sep): Heavy Bay of Bengal rains (~1,150 mm statewide average, peaking July–August). Emerald lawns, wet reflections, and campus umbrellas.
  • Cyclone Squall (Oct–Nov): Peak Bay of Bengal cyclone season (e.g., ITER's Oct 2026 Cyclonic Storm Arnab notice). Slanted rain squalls, swaying palms, and warm lit hall windows.

Regional Odisha Flora

Plantings reflect documented Bhubaneswar campus flora: Krishnachuda / Gulmohar (Delonix regia), Patuli (Lagerstroemia speciosa), Kadamba (Neolamarckia cadamba), Bakul (Mimusops elengi), ornamental Royal Palms, and compact median palms along the entry divider.

Documented SOA / ITER Campus Occasions

  • Chakravyuh & Genesis (Early April): ITER's flagship 3-day techno-cultural festival (held 3–5 April 2026) blending engineering events with concerts on E-Block Lawn, golden string lights, and dusk illumination.
  • SOA Foundation Day (July 17): Anniversary of UGC granting Deemed-to-be-University status to SOA on 17 July 2007—depicted with ceremonial garlands and warm evening lighting.
  • ITER Graduation Day (September): Annual in-person degree conferral at Bansuri Guru Auditorium, depicted with red-carpet welcome canopy and robed graduands gathering on the plaza.
